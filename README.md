# Uncertainty Lab

**Making uncertainty mathematically intuitive.**

Uncertainty Lab is a personal interactive learning project focused on understanding the mathematical foundations of Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence.

The goal is to bridge the gap between abstract mathematical notation and intuitive understanding through interactive visualizations, experiments, and practical exercises.

Rather than simply reading definitions and solving equations, Uncertainty Lab aims to make mathematical concepts tangible and easier to explore.

### Learning Areas

- **Mathematical Foundations** – Set theory, mathematical notation, functions, and Cartesian products
- **Classical Probability** – Probability theory, Bayesian networks, probabilistic graphical models, and inference
- **Understanding Ignorance** – Frames of discernment, refinement, coarsening, and sources of uncertainty
- **Generalized Uncertainty** – Possibility theory, imprecise probabilities, credal sets, and Dempster-Shafer theory
- **Connections & Synthesis** – Exploring relationships between different uncertainty representations

### Philosophy

The project follows a simple principle:

**Understand → Visualize → Experiment → Apply**

The mathematical implementations and visualizations are developed as part of the learning process. The application itself provides the environment and tools to explore these concepts.
